# IELTS Error Taxonomy Reference (v1.0)

Every error detected in learner output must be classified using this standard two-level taxonomy.

---

## 1. Grammar (`grammar`)
- `subject_verb_agreement`: Disagreement in number between subject and finite verb.
- `tense_control`: Incorrect tense choice, unjustified tense shifts, irregular verb forms.
- `article`: Missing, redundant, or incorrect definite/indefinite articles.
- `clause_structure`: Run-on sentences, comma splices, sentence fragments.
- `complex_subordination`: Faulty use of subordinating conjunctions (although...but, because...so).
- `passive_voice`: Inappropriate agentless passive or incorrect auxiliary combination.
- `preposition`: Misuse of dependent or spatial/temporal prepositions.
- `relative_clause`: Incorrect relative pronouns or missing non-defining commas.

---

## 2. Lexical Resource (`lexical`)
- `academic_collocation`: Unnatural word partnerships (e.g., 'do a mistake' instead of 'make a mistake').
- `word_choice`: Inappropriate register, overly informal idioms, semantic imprecision.
- `word_formation`: Incorrect affixation (e.g., 'economical' vs 'economic', 'unaccurately').
- `spelling`: Misspelled academic vocabulary, silent letter omission, double consonant errors.
- `repetitive_vocabulary`: Over-reliance on simple high-frequency descriptors (good, bad, big).

---

## 3. Coherence & Cohesion (`coherence_cohesion`)
- `mechanical_linkers`: Overuse of sentence-initial connectors ('Moreover', 'Furthermore', 'In addition').
- `paragraph_structure`: Lack of clear topic sentences or underdeveloped central ideas.
- `referencing_substitution`: Ambiguous pronoun reference ('this', 'they', 'it') causing confusion.
- `logical_progression`: Abrupt conceptual jumps between adjacent sentences.

---

## 4. Task Achievement / Task Response (`task`)
- `underdeveloped_overview`: (Task 1) Missing or unclear summary of main trends.
- `missing_prompt_component`: (Task 2) Failing to address both sides or direct sub-questions.
- `unclear_position`: (Task 2) Shift in writer's stance between introduction and conclusion.
- `overgeneralisation`: Making sweeping claims without evidentiary support ('all people think...').
- `underlength`: Submission falling below the statutory threshold (150 / 250 words).

---

## 5. Speaking & Fluency (`speaking`)
- `mid_clause_hesitation`: Pausing in the middle of grammatical chunks to search for vocabulary.
- `excessive_self_correction`: Frequently restarting clauses, fragmenting discourse coherence.
- `discourse_signposting`: Inability to structure extended turns across 2 minutes.

---

## 6. Pronunciation (`pronunciation`)
> **Note:** Only assessed when acoustic analysis or audio evaluation is active. Never from text alone.
- `word_stress`: Misplacing primary lexical stress in multisyllabic academic words.
- `sentence_stress_rhythm`: Staccato syllable-timed delivery rather than stress-timed rhythm.
- `connected_speech`: Inability to link final consonants with initial vowels smoothly.
