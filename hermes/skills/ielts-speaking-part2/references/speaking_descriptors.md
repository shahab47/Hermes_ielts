# IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors Reference

## 1. Fluency and Coherence (25%)
- **Band 9:** Speaks fluently with only content-related hesitation; coherent with natural discourse markers.
- **Band 8:** Speaks fluently; occasional self-correction or search for words; develops topics fully.
- **Band 7:** Speaks at length without noticeable effort; some language hesitation or repetition.
- **Band 6:** Willing to speak at length; may lose coherence at times; uses connectives with mixed control.
- **Band 5:** Maintains flow slowly; over-uses certain linkers; simple speech fluent, complex speech hesitant.

## 2. Lexical Resource (25%)
- **Band 9:** Full flexibility and precision in all topics; natural idioms and collocations.
- **Band 8:** Wide resource used flexibly; effective less common idioms with occasional inappropriate choices.
- **Band 7:** Sufficient resource to discuss topics flexibly; uses some less common words and collocations.
- **Band 6:** Wide enough vocabulary to make meaning clear; generally succeeds in paraphrasing.
- **Band 5:** Limited vocabulary; attempts paraphrase with mixed success.

## 3. Grammatical Range & Accuracy (25%)
- **Band 9:** Consistently accurate structures apart from slips.
- **Band 8:** Wide variety of structures; majority of utterances error-free.
- **Band 7:** Range of complex structures; frequent error-free sentences.
- **Band 6:** Mix of simple and complex structures; errors rarely cause comprehension breakdown.
- **Band 5:** Basic sentence forms accurate; complex structures contain errors.

## 4. Pronunciation Policy (25%)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Non-negotiable rule: When assessing from transcribed text without audio analysis, mark Pronunciation as:
> `unassessed (transcript only - audio required)`
