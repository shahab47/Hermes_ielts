# IELTS Speaking Part 2 Cue Cards and Delivery Structure

Part 2 requires a 1-to-2 minute continuous individual monologue based on a task card.

---

## 1. Timing Structure
- **Preparation time:** Exactly 60 seconds (1 minute).
- **Speaking duration:** Aim for 1 minute 45 seconds to 2 minutes.
- **Target word count:** Approximately 180 to 240 words.
- **Speech rate guideline:** 110-140 words per minute.

---

## 2. Note-Taking Strategy (PPF Method)
- **Past:** How the situation began, early background.
- **Present:** Current details, vivid descriptions, specific anecdotes.
- **Future / Feeling:** Why it was memorable, emotional resonance, future reflections.

---

## 3. Cue Card Categories
1. **People:** Family, teachers, mentors, public figures.
2. **Places:** Cities, historical sites, natural landscapes, quiet spaces.
3. **Objects:** Digital devices, gifts, heirlooms, books.
4. **Events:** Celebrations, weddings, cultural festivals, sporting events.
5. **Experiences:** Difficult choices, learning journeys, volunteer work.
