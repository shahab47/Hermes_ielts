# IELTS Grammar Patterns & Micro-Skills Reference

Core patterns for elevating Grammatical Range & Accuracy beyond Band 6.0:

1. **Complex Subordination:** While / Although / Whereas for contrast without double conjunctions.
2. **Conditional Hypotheses:** Past unreal conditionals (If + had + pp, would have + pp).
3. **Passive Structures:** Impersonal passive for formal register (It is generally acknowledged that...).
4. **Participial Clauses:** Participle clauses for economy and sophistication (Recognizing the urgency, governments...).
5. **Nominalisation:** Converting verbs to noun phrases to increase lexical density.
6. **Hedging Modals:** Cautious academic stance (tend to, may substantially).
7. **Negative Inversion:** Rhetorical emphasis (Not only does X, but it also...).
