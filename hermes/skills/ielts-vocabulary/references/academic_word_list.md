# Academic Word List (AWL) & Collocation Principles

For IELTS Band 7.0+, vocabulary must exhibit precision, academic register, and natural collocation.

---

## 1. High-Yield Collocation Patterns
- **Substantial:** substantial increase, substantial proportion, substantial investment, substantial evidence.
- **Fluctuate:** fluctuate wildly, fluctuate between X and Y, seasonal fluctuation.
- **Prevalent:** increasingly prevalent, prevalent attitude, widespread prevalence.
- **Mitigate:** mitigate risks, mitigate climate change, mitigate the adverse impact.
- **Detrimental:** detrimental effect on, highly detrimental, prove detrimental to health.
- **Exacerbate:** exacerbate tensions, exacerbate existing inequalities, exacerbate the crisis.
- **Allocate:** allocate resources, allocate funding, equitable allocation.

---

## 2. Retention Criterion
An item is stored for spaced review ONLY if:
1. It appears as an error in learner output.
2. It represents a high-value collocation relevant to the learner's target topic.
3. The learner explicitly asks to save it.
> Never hoard words that have no pedagogical justification.
