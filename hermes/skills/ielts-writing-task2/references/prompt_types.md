# IELTS Writing Task 2 Essay Prompt Types

Every prompt belongs to one of five core structures. Strategy must align with the prompt requirements:

---

## 1. Opinion Essay (Agree or Disagree)
- **Key instruction:** *To what extent do you agree or disagree?*
- **Required strategy:** Present a clear, unambiguous position in the introduction and maintain it through every paragraph.
- **Structure:**
  - Introduction: Background + Thesis statement (Clear opinion).
  - Body 1: Primary reason supporting your opinion with concrete examples.
  - Body 2: Secondary reason supporting your opinion (or nuanced concession + refutation).
  - Conclusion: Restate thesis using altered vocabulary.

---

## 2. Discussion Essay (Discuss Both Views)
- **Key instruction:** *Discuss both views and give your opinion.*
- **Required strategy:** Both perspectives must be discussed fairly and with depth. Your personal opinion must be identifiable.
- **Structure:**
  - Introduction: Introduce the controversy + State your opinion upfront.
  - Body 1: Examine the perspective you disagree with (or agree with less).
  - Body 2: Examine the perspective you agree with and explain why it is more compelling.
  - Conclusion: Summarize both perspectives and reiterate your verdict.

---

## 3. Advantages & Disadvantages
- **Key instruction:** *Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?*
- **Required strategy:** You must explicitly judge whether the positive aspects carry greater weight than the negatives.
- **Structure:**
  - Introduction: Paraphrase trend + State which side carries greater weight.
  - Body 1: Discuss the advantages.
  - Body 2: Discuss the disadvantages and demonstrate why they are smaller or larger in significance.
  - Conclusion: Reaffirm which side decisively outweighs the other.

---

## 4. Problem & Solution (Causes & Solutions)
- **Key instruction:** *What are the causes of this problem, and what measures can be taken?*
- **Required strategy:** Ensure every solution directly addresses one of the causes identified in Body 1.
- **Structure:**
  - Introduction: Paraphrase problem + Roadmap of causes and countermeasures.
  - Body 1: Analyze 2 distinct causes.
  - Body 2: Propose 2 actionable, realistic solutions directly targeting those causes.
  - Conclusion: Summarize the necessity of taking action.

---

## 5. Direct Questions (Two-Part Question)
- **Key instruction:** *Why is this the case? Is this a positive or negative development?*
- **Required strategy:** Answer both questions with equal paragraph development.
- **Structure:**
  - Introduction: Paraphrase phenomenon + Direct 1-sentence answers to both sub-questions.
  - Body 1: Answer Question 1 thoroughly with reasons.
  - Body 2: Answer Question 2 thoroughly with reasons.
  - Conclusion: Synthesize the answers into a cohesive final statement.
