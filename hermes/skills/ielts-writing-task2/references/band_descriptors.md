# IELTS Writing Task 2 Official Descriptors Guide

## 1. Task Response (25%)
- **Band 9:** Fully developed position; fully addressed all prompt components; extended and well-supported ideas.
- **Band 8:** Well-developed response; relevant, extended and supported main points.
- **Band 7:** Clear position throughout; addresses all parts; main ideas extended and supported (may over-generalize slightly).
- **Band 6:** Addresses all parts (some more fully than others); presents relevant position; some ideas lack development.
- **Band 5:** Addresses task only partially; position not always clear; ideas limited or poorly supported.

## 2. Coherence & Cohesion (25%)
- **Band 9:** Cohesion attracts no attention; skillful paragraphing.
- **Band 8:** Logical sequencing; managed cohesion; appropriate paragraphing.
- **Band 7:** Clear progression throughout; varied cohesive devices used appropriately; clear central topic per paragraph.
- **Band 6:** Overall progression; cohesive devices effective but may be mechanical or faulty; paragraphing present.
- **Band 5:** Some organisation; lacks overall progression; repetitive or inaccurate cohesive devices.

## 3. Lexical Resource (25%)
- **Band 9:** Sophisticated lexical control; rare slips only.
- **Band 8:** Wide range; uncommon lexical items used skillfully; occasional collocation slips.
- **Band 7:** Sufficient range for flexibility and precision; less common lexical items used with awareness of style.
- **Band 6:** Adequate range; attempts less common words with some inaccuracies; spelling does not impede meaning.
- **Band 5:** Limited range; noticeable errors in spelling and word formation.

## 4. Grammatical Range & Accuracy (25%)
- **Band 9:** Full flexibility and accuracy; rare minor slips.
- **Band 8:** Wide variety of structures; majority of sentences error-free.
- **Band 7:** Variety of complex structures; frequent error-free sentences; good control of grammar and punctuation.
- **Band 6:** Mix of simple and complex sentence forms; errors in grammar and punctuation rarely reduce communication.
- **Band 5:** Limited range; complex sentences frequently inaccurate; grammar errors cause comprehension difficulty.
